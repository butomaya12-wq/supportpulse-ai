# Buyer discovery — NOT YET VALIDATED

The published requests found in the research are signals of a class of problem, **not evidence of paid subscriptions**, not customer interviews and not authorized pilot data.

Goal: 5 bounded interviews spanning long-cycle quoting/services and small sales departments. For each, capture: participant/role (with consent), last real missed follow-up, workflow/systems, existing CRM controls, frequency, risk cost evidence, workarounds, specific decision owner, budget and permission to use redacted test data.

Interview questions (ask about facts, not leading preferences):
1. Walk me through the last real deal where an agreed next step was missed.
2. How did you notice, when, and what was the outcome?
3. What controls already exist in CRM? What failed despite them?
4. How often, and which part is actually measured?
5. Who can buy a solution and what budget/decision criteria apply?
6. Can you share a redacted event timeline under consent? If not, don't copy data.

No external outreach has been performed. Real connectors are gated by buyer permission and security assessment.
